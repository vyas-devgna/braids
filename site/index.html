Dynamic thresholding

More risk means more care.

Braids automatically routes your request to the right level of engineering depth. Small fixes skip the ceremony; architectural changes demand evidence.

D3 · Consequence

Threat and failure analysis, independent challenge, real evidence.

Security, data integrity, concurrency, measured performance.

threshold limits 
# smallest change that works, obvious check only
braids low    fix the test, don't redesign

# production shape: failure paths, callers, tests
braids high   (default)

# hostile cases: retry, concurrency, upgrade
braids ultra  migration, rollback, evidence per claim
Seven focused skills

Ask directly, or let Braids step in.

/braids

Right-size and implement, holding all claims to concrete evidence.

/braids-review

Review a diff or PR: identify exactly what breaks and what is unproven.

/braids-audit

Whole repository audit, ranked strictly by consequence × exposure.

/braids-risk

Adversarial pre-mortem. Assume it shipped and broke entirely.

/braids-claims

Maps every subjective "faster" or "secure" claim to its factual evidence.

/braids-depth

Set the engineering threshold explicitly, or ask why a depth was chosen.
