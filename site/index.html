engineering
for the real risk.

Coding agents treat a one-line auth change like a rename. Braids right-sizes the work to the actual risk and refuses to call anything faster, secure, or production-ready without the evidence.

$claude plugin marketplace add vyas-devgna/braids

the difference

depth
Routes work D0 → D4 by consequence, not by line count.
evidence
Every claim mapped to what would prove it. Green tests are not a speed benchmark.
surface
Finds the callers you missed, not just the file you named.
restraint
Prices dependencies and abstractions. “No change” is a valid result.

Portable Agent Skill. No runtime dependency, no MCP server, no telemetry. ~778 tokens resident; loads the rest only when it fires.

two dials

D0 · D1
Local and reversible. Just do it — no research, no ceremony.
D2
Cross-module or platform-sensitive. Model the change surface.
D3
Security, data integrity, concurrency, measured performance. Real evidence.
D4
Irreversible or mission-critical. Migration, rollback, stated residual risk.

low · high · ultra

You set the effort. Depth still follows the risk.

the floor

low never silently weakens auth, privacy, or data integrity.

seven skills, one method

/braids
Right-size and implement, with claims held to evidence.
/braids-review
A diff or PR: what breaks, what is unproven, what costs too much.
/braids-audit
Whole repo, ranked by consequence × exposure.
/braids-risk
Pre-mortem. Assume it shipped and caused an incident.
/braids-claims
Every “faster” and “secure” mapped to its evidence.
/braids-depth
Set the threshold, or ask why a task got its depth.
